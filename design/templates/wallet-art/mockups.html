Your Brand wallet cards: how they will look

Draft 2, 13 Oct 2026: velvet banner made with Higgsfield (ai-backgrounds/bg2.png). Colours from design/DESIGN-GUIDE.md; other banner versions are kept in png/. Real positions are decided by Apple and Google; we supply the colours, images, labels and values.

Apple Wallet: three moments in the life of one card

1. Just joined. 0 points, welcome message on the back.
2. Mid-way. Points and "reward at" show side by side.
3. Reward ready. The card updates itself after the visit and a gold line tells staff and guest.

Google Wallet

Same colours and banner. Google shows the points first, then the banner, then the fields and the QR.

What the guest sees on the card: Your Brand logo, their points, the points left to the reward, their name, their 4-digit code and a QR for the staff to scan.

What changes by itself: points, the reward line, and the banner message when Your Brand sends an offer ("Double points this Friday").

On the back of the Apple card (not shown): terms, support contact, "Powered by LOYALTY", and how to delete your data.

Open points for Saad and Monaim: the changing 4-digit code needs the back end (Google can rotate the QR; Apple needs an update or a "Show my code" page). The Your Brand logo we have is small, so a high-resolution copy from Your Brand will make the card sharper.
