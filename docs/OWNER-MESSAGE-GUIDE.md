# Writing a message to your guests' cards

A short guide for {{CLIENT}}'s owner and managers. A message appears on the lock screen of a guest's phone, from their {{CLIENT}} card. Treat it as a note slipped across the table, not as an advertisement.

## Who receives it
Only guests who agreed to news when they joined. Everyone else is never contacted, and the app never sends to them. Treat a guest's request to stop, or to have their data erased, as urgent (erasure is done from the Data and privacy tab). When in doubt, send less.

## How to write it
- **Length.** The hard limit is 200 characters. Aim for 100 to 140; shorter reads as more confident.
- **The first 40 characters carry the point.** A lock screen cuts the rest. Put the invitation or the news first and the courtesies after: "Join us this Friday evening." not "We hope you are well, and wanted to let you know...".
- **One idea per message.** An event, a reward or a greeting. Never all three.
- **Tone.** Warm, calm and unhurried, like a good concierge. Write "we would be glad to welcome you", not "come and get it". No exclamation marks, no emojis, no capital-letter words.
- **Be specific only when it is true.** Name the day, the time, the dish. If you are not sure, leave it out.
- **Do not repeat a message word for word.** On iPhone the alert only appears when the text changes.

The templates in the app follow all of this. Fill in the blanks, read the preview once out loud, and check that no example sentence is still in it before you send.

## How often, and when
- At most **2 or 3 messages a week**, and fewer is better. A quiet week is fine.
- Send in the late morning or the afternoon, between about 10:00 and 20:00 Bahrain time. **Never at night**, and not in the early morning.
- Space them out: not two on the same day.
- During Ramadan and Eid, keep to greetings and calm invitations, with no sales language.

## What NOT to send
- **A promise {{CLIENT}} has not set.** No reward, bonus, discount, price, menu item or opening time that is not already decided and shown in the app's Rules. If you announce it, honour it.
- **Anything about alcohol.**
- **Anything aimed at children.**
- **Personal data.** No guest names, phone numbers, amounts spent or visit history. Lock screens are visible to people beside the guest.
- **Urgency tricks.** No "hurry", "last chance", countdowns or pressure to decide. If a bonus ends tonight, say when, plainly, once.
- **Anything you would not say to a guest's face.**

## Before you press Send
1. Is every fact in the message true and agreed?
2. Does it read well in 40 characters?
3. Is it under 200 characters?
4. Have I sent fewer than 3 messages this week, and is it daytime?
5. Would I be comfortable if the guest showed it to a friend?

Arabic messages are welcome. Have a native speaker check the wording before the first one goes out.
